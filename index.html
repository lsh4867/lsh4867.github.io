About

I am a third-year undergraduate student in Electrical Engineering at KAIST. In 2026 I was an intern at the Ku Lab, KAIST Graduate School of Medical Science and Engineering (KAIMED Individual Research), working on Schrödinger-bridge diffusion models for unpaired super-resolution of in vivo two-photon microscopy.

I am interested in computer vision and large language models, and in particular in generative models whose outputs stay faithful to the evidence they are given. In microscopy this means a model that sharpens what the optics actually recorded without inventing structure that was never there. I am now looking for opportunities to study these questions in vision and language models more broadly.
