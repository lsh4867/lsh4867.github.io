About

I am a third-year undergraduate student in Electrical Engineering at KAIST. Since February 2026 I have been an undergraduate researcher at the KAIST Graduate School of Medical Science and Engineering, where I develop physics-aware deep learning that restores in vivo two-photon microscopy to expansion-microscopy resolution.

I am interested in computer vision and large language models, and in particular in generative models whose outputs stay faithful to the evidence they are given. In microscopy this means constraining a generator with the optical forward model so that it restores structure without hallucinating it. I am now looking for opportunities to study these questions in vision and language models more broadly.
